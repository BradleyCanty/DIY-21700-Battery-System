## Antispark Printed Circuit Board Assembly (PCBA) Build Instructions

1. Apply solder paste to all the pads on the PCB
2. Populate the PCB with SMD components from the antispark BOM, minus the SMD MOSFETs
3. Solder the SMD components to the board via hot plate
4. Let the board cool down, then flip it over & hand-solder the MOSFETs to their pads
5. Finally, hand-solder the through-hole resistor to its pads & snip its excess legs
